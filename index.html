About

Where data science meets operational reality

My work sits at the intersection of data science, operations, and institutional systems. The hard part is rarely the model. It is the messy data and the decision around it.

For over a decade I worked inside organisations that could not fail: hospitals preparing for JCIA, ISO, and CAP accreditation, and a university network coordinating across East Africa, Central Asia, and Europe. That environment taught me that the bottleneck is almost never intelligence or effort. It is scattered data, manual reporting, and systems no one trusts.

I now solve that as a data scientist. At Aga Khan University's Network of Quality Teaching and Learning, I manage reporting systems, quality frameworks, and institutional documentation, and I led a migration to automated Power BI dashboards that cut reporting cycle time by 40 percent.

Through BriefDesk Solutions, my consultancy, I help schools, NGOs, and institutional teams move from manual workflows to clean data, working dashboards, and decisions they can act on, including deployed AI products now in production.

I am completing a BSc in Data Science at the Open University of Kenya, and I finished the ALX Africa Machine Learning track at 98 percent. I also hold a BSc in Business Studies and an Associate Degree in Health Sciences.
