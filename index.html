About Me

Where data science meets operational excellence

I am a Data Scientist, Analyst, Systems Designer, and Founder based in Nairobi, Kenya. My work sits at the intersection of data science, operations management, institutional systems, product thinking, and decision-support tools.

At Aga Khan University's Network of Quality Teaching and Learning, I support work across East Africa, Central Asia, and Europe — managing data pipelines, reporting systems, quality frameworks, and institutional documentation.

Through BriefDesk Solutions, I help organisations improve workflows, implement practical systems, and build reporting structures that support better decisions.

I am currently completing a BSc in Data Science at the Open University of Kenya, a BSc in Health Sciences at University of the People, and a Machine Learning certification at ALX Africa.

How I Work

A practical process from problem to decision

My work combines technical analysis with operational context, so the final output is useful to both decision-makers and implementation teams.

01

Define the problem

Clarify the operational question, decision need, users, constraints, and success criteria.

02

Structure the data

Clean, organise, validate, and transform messy information into usable datasets and workflows.

03

Analyse and model

Use EDA, statistics, dashboards, and machine learning to identify patterns and test assumptions.

04

Turn insight into action

Translate findings into recommendations, dashboards, systems, documentation, and next steps.
