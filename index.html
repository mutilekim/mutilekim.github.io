About Me

Where data meets operational excellence

I am a Data Scientist, Analyst, and Administrative Systems Consultant based in Nairobi, Kenya. My work sits at the intersection of data science, operations management, and institutional systems design.

At Aga Khan University's Network of Quality Teaching and Learning, I support a team spanning East Africa, Central Asia, and Europe — managing data pipelines, reporting systems, and institutional quality frameworks. Alongside this, I run BriefDesk Solutions, a boutique consultancy delivering data and administrative systems to schools, NGOs, and SMEs.

I am currently completing a BSc in Data Science at the Open University of Kenya, a BSc in Health Sciences at University of the People, and a Machine Learning certification at ALX Africa.
